This app utilizes the React.js library and SASS to deliver a functioning life simulator based on the work of John Conway in 1970. I created this project as part of my personal study of the React JavaScript library developed by Facebook.

AÂ "Conway's Game of Life" simulator such as this one is based on a 2 dimensional grid of squares. Each square can be thought of as a biological cell, a person living on land, etc. At each generation, each square either lives, dies, or comes to life based on its neighbors.

In this implementation, users can add or remove rows and columns, click each square individually to set up their own pattern, or watch a predefined repeating pattern (i.e. a "pulsar").
